Things you can play with

Small interactive pieces built on the Amber engine, compiled to WebAssembly and running on the page in front of you: the real C99 interpreter, not a reimplementation. Each one exists to show a different thing an array language is unreasonably good at. Nothing is installed, and nothing you type leaves your browser.

Dots

A grid of dots drawn by one line of Amber, re-evaluated every frame. No per-pixel callback, because X and Y are whole vectors, so your expression is a single array operation over all 1024 cells.

liveone expression60 fpsgenerative

The Pit

You are the market maker. Order flow hits you twenty times a second and your quote is one line of Amber, re-evaluated on every tick. Quote too tight and informed flow picks you off; quote too wide and you never trade.

livemarket making60-second roundsseeded

Sweep

Minesweeper, with no loop over cells. The whole board's adjacent-mine count is one indexed sum, and opening an empty region is a converge. Play it, then read the forty-five lines that run it.

liveflood fillno loopsmouse

Array Golf

A hundred and fifty-five problems in three tiers, scored in bytes. Write an expression in terms of x; the engine judges it as you type, against every test the puzzle has. The shortest are two characters; the hardest are not.

live155 puzzlesscored in bytesjudged as you type
