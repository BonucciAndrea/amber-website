Run Amber in your browser

The real Amber 2.0.0 engine, compiled to WebAssembly and running entirely on this page — no server, no install. Infix dyads (2 in 1 2), symbol group-by, the join family and bare qSQL all work. Type array code on the left, press Ctrl/Cmd + Enter, and watch it evaluate.

Amber REPL — results render with terminal colour.

Each non-empty line of the editor is fed to the engine in turn, exactly as if typed at the prompt. Pick an entry from Examples… to load its source into the editor — read it, then Run. Everything runs locally in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.
